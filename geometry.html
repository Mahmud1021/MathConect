Lesson 1: Distance Between Two Points

The distance formula helps us find the length between two points.

Formula:
d = √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]

Example:
Find the distance between A(1, 2) and B(4, 6).
d = √[(4 - 1)² + (6 - 2)²]
d = √(9 + 16)
d = √25 = 5 units.

Lesson 2: Midpoint Formula

The midpoint is the point exactly halfway between two points.

Formula:
M = ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)

Example:
Find the midpoint of A(2, 4) and B(6, 8).
M = ((2 + 6)/2, (4 + 8)/2)
M = (4, 6).

Lesson 3: Gradient of a Straight Line

Gradient measures the steepness of a straight line.

Formula:
m = (y₂ - y₁)/(x₂ - x₁)

Example:
For A(1, 2) and B(3, 6):
m = (6 - 2)/(3 - 1)
m = 4/2 = 2.

Lesson 4: Equation of a Straight Line

A common form of a straight-line equation is:

y = mx + c
m = gradient
c = y-intercept

Example: If m = 2 and c = 3, then:
y = 2x + 3.

Important Formulas

  • Distance: d = √[(x₂ - x₁)² + (y₂ - y₁)²]
  • Midpoint: M = ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2)
  • Gradient: m = (y₂-y₁)/(x₂-x₁)
  • Straight line: y = mx + c
