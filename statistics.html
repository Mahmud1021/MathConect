Lesson 1: Introduction to Statistics

Statistics is the branch of mathematics concerned with collecting, organizing, presenting, analysing and interpreting data.

  • Population: The entire group being studied.
  • Sample: A selected part of a population.
  • Data: Facts, figures or observations collected for analysis.
  • Variable: A characteristic that can take different values.

Lesson 2: Mean (Average)

The mean is the sum of all observations divided by the number of observations.

Formula:
Mean = Sum of observations / Number of observations

Example:
Find the mean of 2, 4, 6, 8 and 10.
Mean = (2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10) / 5
Mean = 30 / 5
Mean = 6.

Lesson 3: Median

The median is the middle value when observations are arranged in ascending or descending order.

Example 1: Odd number of observations
Data: 3, 5, 7, 9, 11
Median = 7.

Example 2: Even number of observations
Data: 2, 4, 6, 8
Median = (4 + 6) / 2
Median = 5.

Lesson 4: Mode

The mode is the value that occurs most frequently in a dataset.

Data: 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 3, 6
The value 3 occurs most often.
Mode = 3.

Lesson 5: Range

Range measures the spread between the largest and smallest observations.

Formula:
Range = Maximum value - Minimum value

Data: 4, 7, 9, 12, 15
Range = 15 - 4
Range = 11.

Lesson 6: Variance

Variance measures how far observations are spread around their mean.

Population variance:
σ² = Σ(x - μ)² / N

Sample variance:
s² = Σ(x - x̄)² / (n - 1)

Here, μ is the population mean, x̄ is the sample mean, and N and n are their respective sizes.

Lesson 7: Standard Deviation

Standard deviation measures the spread of data around the mean. It is the positive square root of variance.

Population:
σ = √σ²

Sample:
s = √s²

A smaller standard deviation generally indicates that observations are closer to the mean.

Lesson 8: Frequency Distribution

A frequency distribution organizes data by showing how often each value or class occurs.

Example data: 1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3

Value 1: Frequency 1
Value 2: Frequency 2
Value 3: Frequency 3

Total frequency = 6.

Important Statistics Formulas

  • Mean = Σx / n
  • Range = Maximum - Minimum
  • Population variance = Σ(x - μ)² / N
  • Sample variance = Σ(x - x̄)² / (n - 1)
  • Standard deviation = Square root of variance
  • Relative frequency = Frequency / Total frequency
