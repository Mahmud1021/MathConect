Welcome to Algebra

Learn algebra step by step, from basic expressions to equations.

Lesson 1: Introduction to Algebra

Algebra is a branch of mathematics that uses letters and symbols to represent unknown numbers.

For example:

x + 5 = 12
Here, x represents an unknown number.
Our aim is to find the value of x.

Lesson 2: Algebraic Expressions

An algebraic expression combines numbers, variables and mathematical operations.

Examples:

  • 2x + 3
  • 5a - 4
  • 3x + 2y

Lesson 3: Linear Equations

A linear equation is an equation in which the highest power of the variable is one.

Worked Example:
Solve: 2x + 4 = 12

Step 1: Subtract 4 from both sides.
2x = 12 - 4
2x = 8

Step 2: Divide both sides by 2.
x = 8 ÷ 2

Answer: x = 4

Important Formulas

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
a² - b² = (a - b)(a + b)
